Operating in Argentina

For multinationals with an established Argentine presence. You do not need to be convinced to enter the market — you need to know what changed this quarter, what it means commercially, and who at group level owns the answer.

Who this is for

Groups with an Argentine subsidiary, a country manager, and a general counsel several time zones away who is accountable for a regulatory position they cannot read in the original. Usually the Argentine entity is a small share of revenue and a disproportionate share of the group's regulatory attention.

This is the only one of our three journeys that does not end in a transaction. It ends in a standing arrangement, because the problem is continuous.

The sequence

Six positions to hold, and the failure that most often erodes each one.

Know what changed, and know it in time

A monitoring arrangement that surfaces regulatory movement with its commercial implication attached, rather than as a translated summary of a text.

Where this goes wrong

The change reaches group weeks late, summarised by someone who understood the text but not what it meant for the business. By then the window it opened or closed has moved.

Hold a current exchange and repatriation position

What the regime permits today for your sector and structure, what it requires by way of reporting, and what would change if it moves again.

Where this goes wrong

The position was established once, correctly, and never revisited. Treasury plans against an assumption that expired.

Answered by Regulatory & Compliance Tax

Keep the tax position current on both sides

Argentine corporate and transactional tax, and its interaction with the group's position elsewhere — reviewed rather than assumed stable.

Where this goes wrong

Local advice and group advice are both correct and never meet. The interaction is nobody's file until an audit makes it everybody's.

Answered by Tax

Apply group employment policy where it actually applies

Where group-level assumptions about employment, contractors, benefits and termination hold in Argentina, and where they stop holding.

Where this goes wrong

A global policy is rolled out on the assumption that local law will accommodate it. The gap is usually discovered at termination, which is the most expensive moment to discover it.

Answered by Argentine practice Regulatory & Compliance

Make the intercompany arrangement a decision

How the Argentine entity is funded, how services are priced between entities, and whether that is documented as a position the group would defend.

Where this goes wrong

It accumulated. Nobody decided it, so nobody can explain it — which is a difficult place to be in a transfer pricing enquiry.

Answered by Tax Cross-Border Structuring

Give the group a position it can report

A stated Argentine regulatory position, dated, that the parent's general counsel and board can rely on and can see change.

Where this goes wrong

The board is briefed on Argentina reactively, when something has already happened. A position that is only articulated in a crisis was never a position.

Five questions to answer before you call anyone

Including us. These are diagnostic rather than legal — and if the honest answers are comfortable, you probably do not need an arrangement like this.

  1. How do you currently learn that Argentine regulation has changed, and how long does it take to reach you?
  2. Does the person who reads the change also understand what it means commercially?
  3. When did you last test whether your standing Argentine positions still hold?
  4. Is your intercompany arrangement documented, or habitual?
  5. Who at group level is accountable for the Argentine regulatory position — by name?

Question five is the one most groups cannot answer, and it is usually the reason for the other four.

What a standing arrangement looks like

Typically first

A position review

A fixed-scope assessment of where the group's standing Argentine positions are current, stale or unstated — producing a list, not a retainer proposal.

Then

Monitoring with implication

Regulatory movement reported with its commercial consequence attached, on a stated cadence, to a named person at group level.

As needed

Counsel on the specific

When a change requires action, the legal work is a separate, scoped engagement rather than absorbed silently into a retainer.
