Cross-Border Structuring

Holding structures that survive both tax regimes, both regulators and the diligence of a US investment committee — designed once, rather than corrected later.

What we do

We design and implement the corporate architecture that sits between an Argentine business and United States capital, and between a US business and its Argentine operations. That includes entity selection and formation on both sides, holding company structures, share exchanges and redomiciliation, group reorganisations, intercompany arrangements, and the migration of intellectual property and contracts into the entity that needs to own them.

The work is rarely about choosing a structure. The structures are well known. It is about identifying, before the structure is built, which facts on the Argentine side will be tested from the United States — and fixing them while they are still cheap to fix.

Who we help

AR → US

Companies and founders raising US capital

Establishing a US holding structure that institutional investors will fund, without creating problems in the Argentine entity beneath it.

 US → AR

Investors and acquirers entering Argentina

Choosing the vehicle, the jurisdiction of the holding entity and the route in — with the exit and the repatriation path modelled at the same time.

 AR ↔ US

Groups reorganising existing operations

Restructuring an established Argentine presence — intercompany terms, funding, and the consequences of moving assets between entities.

What problems we solve

  1. Structures that are correct on one side and expensive on the otherA holding arrangement optimised for United States tax efficiency can create an Argentine exposure that nobody modelled, and the reverse is equally common. The two positions have to be built together, in one exercise, before anything is executed.
  2. Intellectual property that was never actually assignedInvestors need the holding company to own what the business runs on. Founders generally assume it does. The gap between those two positions is the single most frequent reason a financing stalls in diligence.
  3. Argentine corporate history read against a US standardMeetings, filings, registrations and the paper trail behind share issuances are assessed at diligence against a standard the company was not applying at the time. Curable in weeks at Series A; a repricing conversation at Series C.
  4. Intercompany arrangements that accumulated rather than were decidedHow the Argentine entity is funded and how services between entities are priced should be a documented decision. When it is instead a habit, it becomes a transfer pricing and permanent establishment question at the worst possible moment.
  5. Exit paths that were never checked against the entryThe structure that is easiest to establish is not always the one a buyer or a listing will accept. Working backwards from the exit is cheaper than rebuilding towards it.

What this means in each jurisdiction

Argentina

Argentina implications

The Argentine operating entity continues to exist and continues to carry its obligations regardless of what sits above it. The questions that matter in practice are whether the historical corporate record supports the shares being exchanged or transferred, whether intellectual property was effectively assigned as it was created, what the transaction itself triggers locally, and how any resulting group arrangement is treated. United States

New York and US implications

The holding entity will typically be governed by Delaware corporate law and financed on documents drafted under New York law. Those documents are the mechanism by which an unresolved Argentine question becomes a US contractual exposure — a representation about title or ownership does not become less enforceable because the underlying facts sit in another jurisdiction and another language. Cross-border

Where the two interact

This is the practice's actual subject. Tax treatment must be modelled jointly rather than as two opinions that do not refer to each other. Securities, exchange and reporting obligations can attach on both sides of the same transaction. And the sequencing matters: several of these steps are difficult to reverse, and the order in which they are taken determines what remains available afterwards.

Most of the value in this work is delivered before anything is signed. By the time a term sheet exists, the options have narrowed.
