01Cross-border

Argentina × New York

The corridor is not a proposition. It is the existing architecture.

Argentine sovereign and corporate debt has for decades been issued under New York law, with submission to the courts of the Southern District of New York. Cross-border financings are documented under it. Argentine founders raising institutional capital do so through structures drafted by New York lawyers.

The legal operating system of Argentine cross-border business is already substantially American. What is missing is not a bridge — it is counsel that understands both ends of one that already carries the traffic.

08Method

How we work

Judgement is demonstrated, not asserted

01

Admitted on both sides

Argentine law is advised by lawyers admitted in Argentina, and United States law by lawyers admitted in New York. We do not blur the two, and we tell you which entity is acting.

02

Counsel and advisory, separated

Legal advice and commercial strategy are different services under different rules. Keeping them structurally apart is what allows us to offer both without misrepresenting either.

03

Published, dated, and reviewed

Our analysis is published under the desk that produced it, cleared by admitted counsel in each jurisdiction it covers, and carries the date its legal position was verified. When that position changes, we say so.
