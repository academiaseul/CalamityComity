Argentina × New York

The corridor is not a proposition. It is the existing architecture.

Argentine sovereign and corporate debt has for decades been issued under New York law. Cross-border financings are documented under it. Argentine founders raising institutional capital do so through structures drafted by New York lawyers. What is missing is not a bridge — it is counsel that understands both ends of one that already carries the traffic.
