Where the corridor is busiest

Five sectors, chosen because they are where Argentine assets and United States capital actually meet — and where a commercial decision becomes a legal question faster than anyone expects.

  • Energy Argentina · Cross-border

    Unconventional oil and gas, power generation and the export infrastructure that determines whether reserves become revenue. The sector where Argentina's investment case is most concrete and its regulatory history most consequential.

    Energy & InfrastructureCapital MarketsRegulatoryTax
  • Mining & Critical Minerals Argentina · Cross-border

    Lithium, copper and the supply chains that industrial policy in the United States and Europe is actively reshaping. Long development horizons, provincial regulation, and offtake structures negotiated a hemisphere away.

    Energy & InfrastructureCorporate & M&ARegulatoryDisputes
  • Infrastructure Argentina · Cross-border

    Transport, logistics, utilities and the assets that connect production to export. Long-horizon investments in a jurisdiction where the horizon itself is the risk being priced.

    Energy & InfrastructurePrivate CapitalRegulatoryDisputes
  • Technology & Digital Economy Cross-border

    Software, fintech and digital services companies built in Argentina and financed in the United States — and US companies building engineering capacity in Buenos Aires. The corridor's highest-volume traffic in both directions.

    Cross-Border StructuringPrivate CapitalTechnology & DataTax
  • Financial Services & Private Capital Cross-border

    Funds, banks and investors allocating across the corridor, and the regimes that govern how they do it — including the New York-law documentation that most Argentine cross-border finance is written on.

    Capital MarketsPrivate CapitalRegulatoryDisputes

How to read these pages

Each industry page follows the same sequence, because it is the sequence in which the problems actually arrive: a commercial reality, the legal question it creates, the regulatory question behind that, and the cross-border question that only appears when the two systems meet.

We have written them to be useful before you engage anyone — including us. If a page tells you what to go and check on Monday, it has done its job.
