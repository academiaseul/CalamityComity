Why we separate them

Most firms present advisory work as an extension of their legal practice. That is convenient and it is misleading: the two are regulated differently, and only one of them is privileged.

Keeping them structurally apart is what allows us to offer both without misrepresenting either — and it means you always know which entity is acting, and whether what you tell us is protected.
