What this template deliberately omits

No photograph. Consistent with the card system, and it removes the single most interchangeable element on a law firm profile.

No rankings, awards or directory listings. Per §35 these are not asserted at all. If the firm later earns them, they belong in one place with a source, not scattered as decoration.

No seniority framing in the headline. The positioning line says what this person is brought in to solve. A title alone tells a client nothing about whether to call them.
